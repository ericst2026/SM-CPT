    Metric definitions

    rel Δ
    ‖W_chat − W_base‖_F / ‖W_base‖_F. The headline “how much did it move” number.
    cosine
    Cosine similarity of the flattened base and chat tensors. 1.0 means same direction.
    mean |Δ| / mean |W|
    Average absolute element change relative to the average absolute base weight.
    max |Δ|
    Largest single-element change.
    changed
    Share of elements whose bf16 value differs at all.
    ‖W‖ base / chat
    Frobenius norm of each checkpoint's tensor.
    top-8 / top-64 energy
    Share of Σσ² of ΔW captured by its 8 / 64 largest singular values.
    rank@90%
    Number of singular directions needed to reach 90% of ΔW's energy.
    eff. rank
    exp(entropy of σ/Σσ), the Roy–Vetterli effective rank of ΔW.
