CPT adapter eval comparison

Four scores per checkpoint: overall MMLU (the forgetting guard-rail), MMLU on the 11 AI-related subjects, the MMLU machine_learning subject, and the in-domain AI MCQ set. Every adapter is scored on the same questions as the base model, so each change is a paired, question-by-question comparison.

Inputs. There is no cpt_qwen06b_rs128_full run. The three “rs128_full” logs are read from runs/cpt_qwen06b_r128_full/eval_logs/, plain LoRA r128, which has exactly epoch 0, 1 and 2. Its epoch0.log evaluates the untouched base model (adapter = None).

Bottom line

  1. Overall MMLU is the only score that separates the adapters. Every adapter forgets, and every drop is significant. rs128_long loses the least (−0.66 pt) and is significantly ahead of each of the other three (p < 0.001 or smaller). ml_rs_adaptive_v1 loses the most (−2.04 pt).
  2. No adapter improves the AI-related MMLU subjects. All four score below base (−0.57 to −1.93 pt on n = 1,761). The order here is the reverse of overall MMLU: rs128_long drops the most and is the only significant drop (p = 0.041). No two adapters differ significantly (every pairwise p ≥ 0.21). rs128_long’s overall advantage comes from the non-AI subjects.
  3. On machine_learning, r128_full ep 1 shows the largest gain, but n = 112 can’t confirm it. It goes 36.6% → 44.6% (+8.0 pt, 95% CI −1.0 to +17.0, p = 0.12), and most of that gain is gone by ep 2 (38.4%). One question on this subject is worth 0.9 pt.
  4. MCQ rises for every adapter, but none of the gains is significant. The gains run +3.2 to +5.9 pt, with ml_rs_adaptive_v1 highest. At n = 186 every 95% CI crosses zero (all p ≥ 0.11), and adapter-vs-adapter gaps are smaller still (all p ≥ 0.49).
  5. What follows from this. The three in-domain signals all point the same weak way: a few points of in-domain gain at best, and none of it resolvable. Of the four adapters, rs128_long ep 0.5 forgets least. No checkpoint shows significant in-domain gain on these four scores. Ranking adapters on in-domain knowledge needs an in-domain MCQ set of roughly 1,000+ questions, where a 3-point paired difference becomes detectable.
